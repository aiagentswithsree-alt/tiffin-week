2nd short break Box 3 · ~3:00 · something to hold them till home
CATEGORIES FOR THIS BOX
Steamed Chickpea, Banana & Paneer Dessert High-protein sweet — good for Box 3 or after school 20 min
INGREDIENTS
Boiled chickpeas Jaggery, melted Cardamom powder Banana, sliced Paneer cubes Whole almonds Pumpkin seeds Grated coconut
METHOD
  1. Drizzle the boiled chickpeas with melted jaggery and cardamom.
  2. Arrange on a banana leaf with the banana, paneer, almonds, seeds and coconut.
  3. Steam for 15 minutes.
Night before: Soak and boil the chickpeas
