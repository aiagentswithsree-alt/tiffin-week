STEAMED · MON SCHOOL BOX 3 Steamed High-Protein Veg Salad Serves 3 · steam 25 min · amounts are estimates
STEAMED BASE Soaked chickpeas¾ cup Paneer cubes150 g Green peas½ cup Raw peanuts¼ cup Pumpkin150 g Capsicum1 Beetroot1 small Sweet potato1 medium Jaggery1 tsp Ghee1 tsp DRESSING Curd1 cup Lemon juice½ lemon Onion1 small Green chilli1 Coriander2 tbsp Saltto taste Chilli flakes½ tsp
METHOD 1Line the steamer plate with a banana leaf. 2Cut pumpkin, capsicum, beetroot and sweet potato into bite-size pieces. 3Spread everything on the leaf; jaggery and ghee on top. Steam 25 minutes. 4Whisk curd, lemon, onion, chilli, coriander, salt and chilli flakes. 5Pour over and mix. For a box, pack the dressing separately.
Night before: soak chickpeas and raw peanuts. Soaked chickpeas stay firm after 25 min — pre-boil if you like them soft.
